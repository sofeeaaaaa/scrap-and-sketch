# Junk Journal Studio

## Experience
- Replace the blank screen with a warm craft-desk workspace centered on a tactile two-page journal spread.
- Keep supply controls compact and visual, using paper tabs, stamps, tape, and hand-drawn marks rather than corporate panels.
- Make the journal usable on desktop and adapt the workspace into a single-page editing view on smaller screens.

## Core editing
- Build a freeform page surface where text, photos, stickers, tape, scraps, tickets, stamps, and doodles can be selected, dragged, resized, rotated, duplicated, deleted, and reordered front-to-back.
- Support direct photo upload, drag-and-drop, and clipboard paste; provide image framing choices and a focused crop-position editor.
- Add visible selection handles and a small contextual tool strip that stays close to the selected item.

## Pages and persistence
- Provide multiple journal pages with add, delete, drag-to-reorder, previous/next navigation, paper selection, and a page-turn transition.
- Autosave the complete journal to this browser and restore it on return.
- Export the active spread as a PNG and the full journal as a PDF using print-ready pages.

## Technical details
- Use browser-native pointer, clipboard, drag/drop, local storage, SVG, and canvas APIs; no account or online storage is required.
- Add focused client-side modules for journal state, editable elements, supply controls, page rail, and export helpers.
- Define the complete tactile palette, typography, shadows, textures, and motion in the shared design system.
- Verify core interactions and layouts in the running preview, including narrow and wide screens.
